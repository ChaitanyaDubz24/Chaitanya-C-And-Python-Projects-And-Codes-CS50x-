# Procrastinot

For many university students, studying takes up a sizable portion of their lives, which often leaves them falling victim to procrastination and digital distractions. Studies have shown that cell phone usage and poor time management are among the leading causes of reduced academic productivity.

To address this, our group developed the Procrastinot, a physical study companion that combines proven time-management techniques with behavioral reinforcement. It uses the Pomodoro technique, timing twenty-five-minute study sessions followed by five-minute breaks to promote sustained focus. To encourage positive study habits, the Procrastinot rewards users with a sweet treat after completing each cycle, and discourages distraction by launching a piece of "trash" at the user when they lose focus. The device also features a "phone jail" compartment that locks away the user's phone, removing a primary source of distraction. When extra motivation is needed, the user can request a randomly generated quote for encouragement.
