# SolidWorks Designs

A collection of parts and assemblies I've modeled in SolidWorks. Each project includes PDF images of the feature tree, views from standard engineering angles, and detailed screenshots of the finished build.

## Contents

1. **Rocketry Part**: a component modeled for rocketry work.
2. **Engineering Part**: a standalone engineering part.
3. **Keychain**: a full keychain shown as a breakdown of its separate parts, followed by the complete assembly.
